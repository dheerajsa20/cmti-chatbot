## Certified courses (CMTI / IDMTI Finishing School)

### Advanced Construction Management Course (CACM)
- Full title: Certificate Course on Advanced Construction Management.
- Duration: 3 months. Modes: offline, online and hybrid. Learning approach: hands-on exercises and construction site visits.
- Skills practised: drawings, BOQ and BBS; construction sequencing; site quality and safety.
- Eligibility: diploma holders, graduates and postgraduates, including freshers and young professionals with one to two years of experience.
- Target audience: aspiring construction professionals who want practical skills in site execution, quantity estimation, quality and safety; also supports people preparing for job interviews or exploring construction entrepreneurship.
- Objective: prepare participants for day-to-day site responsibilities: interpreting drawings and specifications, understanding construction materials, performing calculations, estimating quantities and costs, and sequencing construction activities.
- Learning outcomes: interpret drawings and specifications, convert units and take site measurements; identify cement, aggregates, reinforcement steel and masonry units and their properties, uses and basic quality checks; calculate area, volume, material quantities and reinforcement weight; prepare Bills of Quantities (BOQs), Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) and rate analyses; sequence construction activities including formwork and site execution procedures; identify quality checks and safety practices; use AutoCAD, SketchUp and BIM tools; prepare for job interviews and the first steps towards a construction business.

### Interior Design & Management Course (IDM)
- Full title: Certificate Course on Interior Design & Management, offered by IDMTI (Interior Design & Management Training Institute), founded by Er. S. G. Ashok Kumar.
- Duration: 3 months. Modes: offline, online and hybrid. Includes hands-on exercises, software training and field visits to modular factories, showrooms and interior project sites.
- Alumni have worked with firms including HomeLane, DesignCafe, Bonito Designs and Purva Streaks; 200+ interior firms have hired IDMTI alumni over the past 8 years.
- Eligibility: diploma holders, graduates and postgraduates, including freshers and young professionals with one to two years of experience.
- Target audience: aspiring interior design professionals and early-career professionals interested in large-scale interior projects.
- Syllabus: interior design standards, drawings and specifications; materials and hardware selection for interior and modular works; AutoCAD, SketchUp and an introduction to BIM; quantity estimation, basic costing and modular cut lists; client requirements, design presentations and feedback; factory production, showroom design, material selection and site installation.
- Learning outcomes: prepare AutoCAD layouts and SketchUp 3D models; interpret specifications and select materials and hardware; calculate quantities and prepare estimates and cut lists; explain how drawings guide manufacturing and installation; document client requirements, present options and respond to feedback.

## For Corporates
- Technical capability development: customised construction training for teams, project needs and industry standards.
- Top 5 reasons companies choose CMTI: customised programmes (aligned with L&D and Operations); experienced industry practitioners (50+ trainers, mostly former L&T); pan-India delivery at project sites and offices, with online and hybrid options; affordable pricing (scope, duration and format aligned to budget); proven track record and strong NPS (real estate, EPC and government clients; feedback tracked with Net Promoter Score).
- How programmes are customised: discuss needs with L&D and Operations; identify skill gaps and map competencies; refine and agree the session plan; develop tailored training content; deliver and assess learning.
- Capability Development Programme for Staff, by career stage:
  - Freshers: Diploma Engineer Trainee (DET) and Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET) programmes, tailored to business needs; CMTI has delivered GET programmes for nine years (details on request).
  - Middle management (3 to 15 years of experience): a two-day workshop on construction topics selected with the client is recommended.
  - Construction and project managers (15+ years): workshops tailored to senior responsibilities and organisational priorities.
- Training topics: Construction Management (core and shell, formwork, finishes, handover protocols); Project Management (planning, project controls, software tools); Quality Management; Safety Management; MEPF (mechanical, electrical, plumbing and firefighting); AI in Construction; Real Estate for non-real-estate professionals; Finance for non-finance professionals; Customer Relationship Management; RERA and National Building Code; Green Buildings, Sustainability and ESG; Quality Management Systems (QMS: ISO 9001:2026 requirements, audit planning, auditing principles, audit reporting and the third-party certification process).
- Capability Development Programme for Workmen: formwork and scaffolding (certification available), masonry, bar bending, plumbing, waterproofing and finishing works, customised to project needs by experienced trade trainers.
- Content Development: SOPs and training content for in-house learning systems, tailored to project workflows, roles and site requirements.
- Assessment of Employees: periodic assessments using suitable internationally used tools, with reports on competency gaps and training priorities.
- Hiring Support: help coordinating campus hiring for entry-level engineering roles, drawing on 5,000+ trained engineers and 100+ partner colleges.
- Clients include DLF, Godrej Properties, Hines, ISRO, L&T Institute of Project Management, Larsen & Toubro, M3M, Prestige Group, Sattva Group, Signature Global, Tata Projects, Adarsh Group, Birla Estates, Shapoorji Pallonji, Piramal Realty, KEC International and others.
